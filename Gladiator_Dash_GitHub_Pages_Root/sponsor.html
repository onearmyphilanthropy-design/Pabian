Who it helps

Still Creek Ranch takes in children out of crisis, abuse, neglect, abandonment and trafficking, and raises them in family-style homes where they can recover. It has been doing that since 1988.

One Army does not just write a cheque. Members volunteer at the Ranch through the year. The race is how they fund it.

One Army at the Still Creek Ranch gate, handing over the year's proceeds.

Race day

A 5K through the mud at Lake Bryan, obstacles throughout, one swim station, then live music and food. Every obstacle is optional, so it draws first-timers and competitors alike.
